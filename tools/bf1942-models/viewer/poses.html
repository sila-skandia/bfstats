Grip inspector

Soldier
Weapon
Arrow keys: left/right steps the weapon, up/down the soldier.

Stance

Keys 1/2/3 switch stance. Older extracts carry only the standing pose; the other two are disabled for those.

Motion

Keys Q/W/E. Idle is the stance's breathing still; walk and run play the game's own .baf timeline at the rate the engine plays it. Kneeling and prone have one moving clip each (Lb_CrouchForward, Lb_LieForward), so walk and run land on the same gait there, as they do in the game.

left palm to weapon—
unwelded (at origin)—
Left palm only lands on two-handed grips; rifles at StandAim carry diagonally with the left arm down, so a large left number there is the game's stance, not a weld error.

Fire

Hold to fire the loaded weapon from its own muzzle node — the same GunFire runtime the model browser uses. Rounds are the game's own hit-test bullets, so bursts are legible through the round counter and the recoil kick rather than a visible projectile.
