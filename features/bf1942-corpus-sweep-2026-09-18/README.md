## The three structural absences (confirmed across audits)

1. **102 of 216 EffectBundles produce zero geometry** — every smoke, fire, dust, debris, damage effect. The emitter data model is 80% unparsed (`intensity`, spread cones, flipbooks, drag). — **RESOLVED 2026-09-25**: `bf42/effects.py` fully parses intensity/curves/flipbooks/drag; `vehicle-emitter-bake.md` widened `_effect_emitter_nodes` to bake alpha sprites, nested bundles and debris, closing ~76 of the 101 empty bundles (remaining ~25 are legitimately sound-only); `viewer/effects.js` plays smoke/debris/decals with full blend-mode support, no viewer changes were even needed.
2. **No soldier geometry in any level** — `soldierSpawns` renders as 2D minimap dots only. — **RESOLVED 2026-09-25**: kit soldiers are now placed/rendered (spawning.js, soldier-body.js), plus bots (bot.js et al.).
3. **Nothing beyond a round collides** — projectiles stop at terrain/sea/hull (closed), but tanks, jeeps, debris, and infantry do not. — **PARTIAL 2026-09-25**: Vehicle-vs-vehicle rigid-body contacts (`body-contact.js`, `body-statics.js`, `body-world.js`) and vehicle-vs-statics/terrain closed 2026-09-20 (`features/vehicle-collision-physics/`). Infantry (`SoldierBody`/`soldier-resolve.js`) collides with terrain and static hulls via its own resolve, but is not wired into the mutual `BodyWorld` contact solver, so it still passes through vehicles — parity-gaps.md itself still lists "the soldier as a body" as open. Debris (`effects.js`) remains a cosmetic particle effect with no physical collision at all.
