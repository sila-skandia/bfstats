3. **Nothing beyond a round collides** — projectiles stop at terrain/sea/hull (closed), but tanks, jeeps, debris, and infantry do not. — **PARTIAL 2026-09-25**: Vehicle-vs-vehicle rigid-body contacts (`body-contact.js`, `body-statics.js`, `body-world.js`) and vehicle-vs-statics/terrain closed 2026-09-20 (`features/vehicle-collision-physics/`).
