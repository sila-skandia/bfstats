# Images for the 2026-11-12 retail-parity cross-check

"Before" is commit `4bc1f4603`, "after" is `faa39811f`. Each tree was exported with
`git archive <commit> tools/bf1942-models | tar -x`. Every chart's numbers come from
running that tree's own modules in node 22. The only exceptions are the two charts
that plot tables from the buckets (M1A1, DICE). The charts are hand-built SVGs on a
light surface: orange is before and blue is after (the dataviz palette's slots 1 and 2,
checked with its validator).

The screenshots come from `serve_viewer.py` running one port per tree, with missing
assets fetched from mesh.bfstats.io. Pages were driven headless with Playwright (`channel:
'chrome'`) through the viewer's own test hooks (`window.__deploy`, `__teleport`,
`__bailOut`, `__lookDelta`, `__botCtl`, `__botBodies`), on `map.html?...&shots=1&mute`,
with frames stepped by `__renderOnce`. No live page was opened.

## Skipped

- **`s-bazooka-cone-before.png` / `-after.png` (DEV-8): not made, because the two frames are identical.** The vanilla `BazookaTrigger` and `PanzershreckTrigger1` blocks in the content store carry `minDeviation 0.0`, `maxDeviation 0.5` and no ordinary deviation words. So before, the floor was already 0 and the lid had nothing to clamp: the live page reports `deviationDeg 0` on both servers, and the crosshair is closed in both frames (checked on El Alamein with `weapon=Bazooka`). None of the 1,144 cached weapon glbs has a `minDeviation` above 0. A template with a positive `minDeviation` (from a mod) would show the difference, but none was available locally.
- **`s-tank-reload-before.png` / `-after.png` (VHUD-10): not made, because the frames would not differ visibly.** A live El Alamein Sherman's `Ammo/ReloadTime` HUD value, sampled every frame after a shot on both servers, follows the same linear fill. The two differ only in the last ~0.1 s, by at most a few percent of the bar. The difference is shown in `c-reload-bar.svg` instead.
